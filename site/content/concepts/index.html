How to read the confidence labels

Some of these concepts are mechanisms — they work by construction, and the evidence is that the mechanism does what it says. Others are claims about outcomes, and those carry an evidence state: supported, provisional, contradicted or open. The two are never merged. A contract Ordo requires can be adopted while the benefit it predicts remains unproven, and where that is the case, this site says so on the page.
