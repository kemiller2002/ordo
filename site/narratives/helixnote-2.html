Interpretation

This experiment succeeded at what it set out to do and, in the same run, refuted the thing everyone expected it to confirm.

The success is real: five small mechanisms, none of them an architectural upheaval, took the targeted blind spots from two thirds mechanically discoverable to all of them. One of the five did something better still — making the wire discriminator single-sourced removed the site rather than mechanising its discovery. That is the only instance in this whole evidence base of construction optimisation rather than detection optimisation, and it is the one worth imitating.

The refutation is that boundary amplification did not move at all. Four boundary files per semantic decision before hardening, four after. This is the measurement that separates two claims people habitually merge: making required work easier to find, and making less work required. Only the first happened.

And then Phase 20, which is the reason this programme is careful. An out-of-scope mutation deliberately re-created Experiment 1's failure shape against the newly hardened architecture. Exhaustive dispatch guaranteed the arm existed. Nothing guaranteed the arm did anything, and nothing mechanical noticed that it did not.

Read together: hardening works, for the thing hardening does. The gap it leaves is not a gap in the hardening — it is a different class of failure that needs a different class of mechanism.
