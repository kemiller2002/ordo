Interpretation

This is the most useful experiment in the programme and the one that looks worst in a summary.

Set against its own predictions, it mostly failed. Of six preregistered hypotheses, three were contradicted outright and one came out mixed. The hardened condition's headline discovery rate went down. And the figure that would have been the programme's best marketing asset — a 68% token reduction — cannot be claimed at all, because the run that produced it was interrupted and resumed, and the counted portion began after most of the work was already done.

What makes it valuable is that it separated two things the programme had been treating as one. Mechanical discovery rate had been serving as a proxy for cost. In this trial the hardened condition had a lower discovery rate and lower search and repair costs simultaneously. A proxy that moves opposite to the thing it proxies is not a proxy.

The confounders are worth reading in full, because they are the model for how this kind of trial should be reported. Different investigation strategies between the two agents. Different self-classification strictness, which the experiment noticed mid-flight and responded to by introducing a second measure rather than by picking the flattering one. Nineteen minutes of one condition's wall clock spent on builds the other never ran. Warm caches for whichever condition went second. A shared database. Each of these is recorded as a reason to distrust the numbers, by the people who produced the numbers.

There is one unambiguously good result buried in it: across the entire independent re-verification, every build, test and architecture-check result either agent reported was reproduced exactly against their committed diffs. Zero discrepancies. Both agents also correctly reported their own token metrics as unavailable rather than estimating them. That says something about whether agent self-reports can be trusted as evidence, and the answer here was yes — which is only worth knowing because somebody checked.

And the semantic no-op appeared again, identically, in both conditions, caught by integration tests only. Third trial, third time.
